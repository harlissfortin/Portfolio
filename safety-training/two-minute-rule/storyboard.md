## 1. cold-open-title

**Purpose.** Hook without fear: name the gap between the plan written earlier and the job as it is now, then land the title.

**Visual.** GLOBAL STYLE (whole video): 1920x1080 flat vector, no photos. Teaching scenes use a warm off-white field with navy type. Case scenes (6 to 8) shift to muted slate with off-white type and slower motion, with no stings. People are neutral grey-blue rounded silhouettes with no facial features, never colour-coded. The four question cards each keep one non-traffic hue and one icon all video: Q1 blue, Q2 violet, Q3 teal, Q4 plum. The three decision buttons are always green 'Go', amber 'Fix it, if safe' and red 'Stop & get help', in every scene including the recap and closing card. Muted red appears otherwise only on hazard labels. No author or person's name and no logo appears mid-video; sources live on the end card. From scene 2 on, a small chapter header with a stopwatch icon sits top-left. SCENE: s1-1: a flat document icon labelled 'PLAN' slides in from the left onto a desk line, with a small tag 'Written earlier'. Its sketch shows a pipe, a CLOSED valve and a small vessel. s1-2: the screen splits. ## 2. what-it-is — What it is

**Purpose.** Define the rule: a short pause you take where you do the work, before starting and again when things change. It adds to SOPs and permits and never replaces them. Two minutes is a rule of thumb, and it is not the productivity tip.

**Visual.** s2-1: a neutral silhouette stands at a simple workstation (a boxy machine with a control panel). A large two-bar pause icon drops in above it and settles. Text: 'A deliberate pause' / 'At the point of work'. s2-2: a horizontal shift timeline draws left to right beneath. Small pause icons pop onto four markers in time with the narration: 'Before you start', 'After a break', 'After handover', 'Any change'. s2-3: four document blocks stack up like a wall at left: 'SOP', 'Permit', 'Lockout', 'Confined space'. A thin glowing layer labelled '2-minute pause' slides onto the top with a small '+'. Nothing in the stack is crossed out. Text: 'Adds to. Never replaces.' s2-4: a stopwatch reading '2:00' appears. A hand-drawn tilde stamps in front of it ('~2:00') with the label 'Rule of thumb', then the face dissolves into the plain pause icon. Nothing else is added on this beat. s2-5: a generic to-do checklist card labelled 'Productivity tip' slides in at right. A neat stamp 'Not this rule' lands on it, and it slides away.

| Cue | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| `s2-1` | It's a short, deliberate pause you take where you do the work. | A deliberate pause · At the point of work |
| `s2-2` | Take it right before you start, and again after a break, a handover, or any change. | Before you start · After a break · After handover · Any change |
| `s2-3` | It adds to your SOPs, permits, lockout, and confined-space programs. It never replaces them. | Adds to. Never replaces. |

## 4. how-it-works — How it works

**Purpose.** Teach the method as one memorable set: four moves (stop, look, ask, decide), four short questions, and three decisions. These return all through the video.

**Visual.** s4-1: four large tiles drop in left to right, each on its spoken word: STOP (raised hand), LOOK (eye), ASK (question mark), DECIDE (signpost). s4-2: the STOP tile pulses and a gloved hand lifts away from a valve ('Hands off'). Then the LOOK tile pulses and scan rings spread around a small job icon and the area beside it ('Look around, and next to it'). s4-3: the ASK tile opens into a header 'Four questions', and two cards flip in side by side, each with its number, hue and icon: '1 What's different?' (blue, two overlapping documents with a small mismatch mark) and '2 What could hurt us?' (violet, a hazard triangle with two small silhouettes). s4-4: cards 3 and 4 flip in: '3 Checked or assumed?' (teal, a circle split between a tick and a question mark, with a small valve-and-lock glyph to show it is about controls) and '4 What if it goes wrong?' (plum, a branching arrow). s4-5: the DECIDE tile splits into three buttons that light in order with the narration: green 'Go', amber 'Fix it, if safe', red 'Stop & get help'. The four cards then shrink and dock in a vertical question rail on the left edge. The rail stays on screen through scene 5, hides for scene 6, and returns in scenes 7 and 8.
