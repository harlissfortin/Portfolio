## 1. title-open — Intro

**Purpose.** Hook on three everyday pharma tasks. Freeze the moment before the hands move, respect what the viewer already knows, and name the rule.

**Visual.** (~12 s) Light theme on a warm off-white background. s1-1: three white cards pop in across the top, left to right, one per phrase. Each card is a single icon over a label: an amber gear 'Clear a jam', a blue wrench 'Break a line' and a teal lock 'Open a vessel'. s1-2: the cards dim to about 30%, and the neutral silhouette fades in at centre stage below them. On 'Before', a raised hand icon pops beside the silhouette's shoulder. On 'give', an amber ring draws once, clockwise, around the hand. On 'minutes', the label 'Two minutes' fades in to the right of the ring. s1-3: the cards fade out, followed by the silhouette, hand, ring and label. A stopwatch reading '2:00' pops in at top centre, and its amber ring sweeps once around the dial. The title 'The 2-Minute Rule' rises in beneath the stopwatch, and the subtitle 'Six saves on a pharma floor' rises in below it. Hold for about 1 s.

## 2. the-rule — The rule

**Purpose.** Define the rule: a short, deliberate pause at the point of work, right before you start and again after any break, handover, interruption or change. Two minutes is a rule of thumb, and what matters is the pause.

**Visual.** (~14 s) Light theme. s2-1: the title 'A pause at the point of work' rises top-left, with an amber pin icon at its left. On 'point', a thin timeline grows left to right across the middle of the stage. s2-2: markers pop along the line, one per spoken item. Each is an amber-ringed circle with an icon and a label beneath: pause icon 'Before you start' at the left end, then clock 'Break', people 'Handover', phone 'Interruption' and gear 'Change'. s2-3: the timeline and markers fade. A large stopwatch pops in at left, its amber ring full and reading '2:00'. On 'rule of thumb', the heading 'Rule of thumb' rises to its right and the '2:00' digits fade, leaving the empty dial. On 'What matters', the line 'The pause is what matters' rises beneath the heading. The title stays at the top throughout.

## 3. line-clearance — Line clearance for people

**Purpose.** Anchor the rule in a habit the audience already owns: line clearance for product, right before each run (21 CFR 211.130(e), shown on screen as a citation chip and not read aloud). The 2-minute rule is line clearance for people, shown on the same packaging line that was just cleared. It adds to SOPs, permits, lockout and engineering controls such as guards and interlocks, and never replaces them. It catches what the plan missed because the worker is at the job.

**Visual.** (~18 s) Light theme. s3-1: the heading 'Line clearance: product' rises top-left, and the packaging line wipes in: a blister packer at the left end of a long conveyor. ## 4. how-to-run-it — Stop · Look · Ask · Decide

**Purpose.** Teach Stop · Look · Ask · Decide with the four questions and the three decisions, and build the question rail and decision buttons that every save reuses. Set the limit on fixes once, where the decision is defined: a fix must be one you are trained and authorized for and that is in your procedure; anything else is Stop & get help.

**Visual.** (~19 s) Light theme. The STOP / LOOK / ASK / DECIDE tiles drop in, unlit, in a row across the top, and each tile lights amber while it is being taught. There is no silhouette in this scene. s4-1: STOP lights (hand icon), and the sub-label 'Hands off' rises under it. s4-2: LOOK lights (eye icon). On 'around', the two-line sub-label 'Around it · Next to it' / 'Against the plan' rises under it. s4-3: ASK lights and both sub-labels fade. The question cards pop into a 2 x 2 grid beneath the tiles as each question is spoken: card 1 (blue, docs icon) 'What’s different?' and card 2 (violet, warning icon) 'What could hurt us?'. s4-4: card 3 (teal, check-and-question icon) 'Checked or assumed?' and card 4 (plum, branch icon) 'What if it goes wrong?' pop in below them. s4-5: DECIDE lights, the four cards fade, and three buttons pop in a centred row, each on its words: green 'Go', amber 'Fix it, if safe' and red 'Stop & get help'. On 'in your procedure', the sub-label 'Only if trained, authorized & in procedure' sets under the amber button (the button label itself does not change). Just after the red button appears, the sub-label 'Get help: your lead' sets under it.

## 5. six-saves — Six saves

**Purpose.** Set the base rate: most pauses end in Go, and on a repeat task with nothing different the pause takes seconds. Catches are a small minority, with no ratio implied. Introduce six saves, ordered by the question that caught each, and say once that they are illustrative. Set up the save template.

**Visual.** (~15 s) Light theme. s5-1: the heading 'Most pauses end in Go' rises top-left. A shift timeline runs across the middle of the stage. A dense run of more than 90 small grey ticks, too many to count, flips green in a fast wave from left to right. Six larger amber-outlined circles, irregularly spaced among the ticks, stay outlined. On 'repeat', a second line 'Repeat task: seconds' rises under the heading. A legend fades in under the timeline: a green tick 'A pause that ended in Go' and an amber ring 'A pause that caught something'. s5-2: on 'catches', the six amber rings pulse once. Just before 'Here are six saves', the heading, timeline and legend fade out. On 'Here', the title 'Six two-minute saves' rises top-left, and six numbered tiles pop in turn into a 3 x 2 grid. Each tile has a left stripe and number in the colour of the question that caught it, an icon, a title and a trigger chip: 1 gear 'Tablet press', 'Handover' (blue); 2 bolt 'Powder charge', 'Before you start' (violet); 3 O₂ cloud 'Inerted vessel', 'Change · Campaign' (violet); 4 lock 'Blister packer', 'Interruption' (teal); 5 shield 'Potent API', 'After a break' (teal); 6 wrench 'Line break', 'Before you start' (plum). s5-3: a small grey tag fades in under the grid: 'Illustrative composites, built from common pharma hazards'. In every 'Stop & get help' save, the people icon that arrives is labelled 'Your lead'. A green Go chip then joins the decision chip with an arrow. Light theme throughout.

## 6. save-1-tablet-press — Save 1 · Tablet press handover
