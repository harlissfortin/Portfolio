# Yellow Belt — Contributing to Improvement Projects

**Format:** Hybrid-lite — self-paced e-learning + 2 live virtual workshops
**Duration:** ~12–14 hours over 2–4 weeks (8–10 hrs self-paced, 2×90-min workshops, plus
mini-project time in the learner's own work)
**Prerequisites:** White Belt certificate, or the 1-hour bridge module (fee-waived)
**Credential:** Certified Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt — exam **and** rubric-reviewed real
mini-project required. 1.2 IACET CEUs (pending accreditation).

## Course structure

| Component | Content | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Unit 1** | Process Thinking: SIPOC & Process Mapping | 2.0 h self-paced |
| **Workshop 1** | Process Mapping Clinic (live, virtual) | 1.5 h live |
| **Unit 2** | Workplace Organization: 5S, Visual Management & Standard Work | 1.5 h self-paced |
| **Unit 3A** | Data Basics I: Definitions & Collection | 1.0 h self-paced |
| **Sim (collect)** | Collect 20 orders on your own check sheet — at least one day after 3A | 0.5 h embedded |
| **Unit 3B** | Data Basics II: Pareto, Run Charts & the Numbers You'll Be Shown (center/spread, reading control charts, capability & MSA awareness) | 1.5 h self-paced |
| **Unit 4** | Root Cause: Problem Statements, 5 Whys & Fishbone | 1.5 h self-paced |
| **Workshop 2** | Root-Cause Simulation (live, virtual) | 2.0 h live |
| **Unit 5** | Improvement in Action: PDCA, Just-Do-It, Kaizen Participation & Your Role on DMAIC Teams | 1.5 h self-paced |
| **Sim (analyze & PDCA)** | Pareto, cause analysis, and countermeasure cycle on your sim data | 0.5 h embedded |
| **Exam** | 40 questions from a 140-item tagged bank; provisional 75% cut (Angoff pending); 2 retakes | 1 h |
| **Mini-project** | Real Just-Do-It improvement, A3-lite storyboard, instructor-reviewed | learner's workplace |

Plus a **12-item pre-assessment** before Unit 1 (test-out of Units 1–2 at 10/12; pre/post
gain reported per cohort) and **cumulative checks** closing Units 2, 3A, 3B, 4 and 5.
Total ≈ 14–15 hours over **a minimum of three weeks** — the units are released on a
schedule (no binge path), because spacing is part of the learning design.

Recommended sequence: Unit 1 → Workshop 1 → Unit 2 → Unit 3A → *(gap)* → Unit 3B →
Unit 4 → Workshop 2 → Unit 5 → exam → mini-project submission (may trail by up to 60
days; certification issues when both gates pass).

Individual enrollees join the monthly public workshop sessions; corporate cohorts get private
sessions (or the 2-day on-site bootcamp variant that compresses all units and workshops).

## Certification gates
1. **Exam:** 40 questions (≥50% scenario-based, ≥50% Apply/Analyze) assembled to blueprint
   from a 140-item tagged bank; provisional **75%** cut pending the modified-Angoff panel;
   two retakes included with ≤ 25% item overlap. Item bank: [`exam-bank.md`](exam-bank.md).
   Policy: [`../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md`](../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md).
2. **Mini-project:** a real improvement in the learner's own work area, documented on the
   A3-lite storyboard and scored by a certified instructor against the published rubric —
   **pass = 7/10 with no dimension scored zero.**
   Template: [`mini-project/storyboard-template.md`](mini-project/storyboard-template.md) ·
   Rubric: [`mini-project/rubric.md`](mini-project/rubric.md).

## Design notes for content/production team
- Units are written as teach-text; storyboard each into 15–20-min interactive segments with
  the same Hook → Teach → Show → Try pattern as White Belt.
- All examples ship in three vertical flavors (manufacturing / healthcare / transactional);
  the units below carry the skeleton with examples from each.
- The virtual simulation (one full improvement cycle with real collected data) is embedded
  across Units 3A–5: learners collect check-sheet data in the sim (after 3A), chart it
  (3B), analyze causes (Unit 4), and run a PDCA countermeasure (Unit 5).
- **Body-of-knowledge alignment:** Unit 3B was added so that the Yellow Belt covers the
  descriptive statistics, control-chart reading, and capability/MSA awareness items in the
  IASSC and ASQ Yellow Belt bodies of knowledge — at *reading* depth, not computation. The
  published crosswalk should list these as "awareness/interpretation" for this level.
