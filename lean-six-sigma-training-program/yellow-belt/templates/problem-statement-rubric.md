# Problem Statement Rubric

*Used in Unit 4, Workshop 2, the exam, and the mini-project rubric (dimension 1). Tape it
to your monitor.*

## Must contain — the five facts
| Element | Test question | Example fragment |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | What is wrong, specifically? | "…34% of discharges occur after 4 pm…" |
| **Where** | Which process/area/product? | "…on Unit 4…" |
| **When / how often** | Since when? What frequency? | "…since January… (120/mo baseline)…" |
| **How big** | Measured, or honestly estimated with the basis shown | "…against a noon target…" |
| **So what** | Impact on customer, cost, time, safety | "…delaying ED admissions a median 3.1 h." |

## Must NOT contain — the three smugglers
| Smuggler | Sounds like | Why it's fatal |
|---|---|---|
| **A cause** | "Because the scanner is outdated…" | Ends the investigation before it starts; the statement must be agreeable to every camp regardless of their pet theory |
| **Blame** | "…night shift keeps mislabeling…" | Drives truth underground; problems get hidden from you forever after |
| **A solution** | "We need a discharge coordinator…" | The meeting is over and nothing was investigated. **Watch for the disguise:** "lack of X" / "no X" = "the solution is X" ("lack of training," "no automation") |

## Self-check sequence
1. Read it aloud to someone who disagrees with you about the cause. Can they sign it? If
   not, a smuggler is hiding in it.
2. Circle every number. Zero circles → go get a baseline (even a week of tallies).
3. Search it for "because," "due to," "lack of," "no [noun]," and any person/team name used
   as an explanation. Remove what you find; state only the gap.
4. Could it end with "…and we don't yet know why"? A true problem statement always can.

## Calibration examples
❌ *"Because we're short-staffed, discharge is always a mess and we need a coordinator."*
✅ *"Since January, 34% of Unit 4 discharges (baseline 120/mo) occur after 4 pm against a
noon target, delaying ED admissions by a median 3.1 hours."*

❌ *"AP errors are out of control due to lack of attention to detail."*
✅ *"In Q2, 9.8% of manually entered invoices (n=3,120) required correction after approval
rejection, adding a median 1.8 days to payment and ~11 staff-hours/week."*
