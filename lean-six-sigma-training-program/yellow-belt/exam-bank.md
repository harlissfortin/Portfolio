# Yellow Belt Certification Exam — Item Bank (v2)

**Governed by:** [`../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md`](../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md)

**Administration:** 40 items per form, 60 minutes, assembled to the blueprint from the CERT
pool (140 items — the 3.5× minimum for a 40-item form). Provisional cut **75%** pending the
first modified-Angoff panel. Two included retakes; each retake form shares ≤ 25% of items
with prior attempts and never the items previously missed.

**Minimally competent candidate (draft for the Angoff panel):** *Can serve reliably on a
DMAIC team — map a real process at the right altitude, write a cause-free problem
statement, collect and chart data honestly, read a run chart and a control chart without
over-reading noise, contribute checkable causes and verify them, and run a small PDCA on
their own work — but would not be expected to lead a project, build a control chart, or
select a statistical test.*
