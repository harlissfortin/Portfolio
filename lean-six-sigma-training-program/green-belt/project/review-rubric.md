# Green Belt Project — Review Rubric (100 points)

**Pass = 75 points with every mandatory item earned.** Scored by a certified Black Belt or
Master Black Belt reviewer who is **not** the learner's instructor or coach (separation of
coaching and assessment). Feedback within 15 business days; one revise-and-resubmit cycle
included. Published to learners in week 1: the project is run *toward* this rubric.

**Mandatory items** (★) are pass/fail regardless of total: a project scoring 90 without a
verified root cause does not pass. That asymmetry is the credential.

Practicum-track projects are scored on the same rubric against the simulated dataset;
sponsor items are scored by the practicum instructor acting as sponsor and the credential is
labeled accordingly.

---

## Define — 15 points

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| D1 Problem statement | 4 | What, where, since when, how big, why it matters; no cause, blame or solution; the same statement everyone on the team can sign |
| D2 Charter and scope | 4 | Sponsor-signed; start/stop points; in/out of scope; team and roles; timeline; the primary metric named with its operational definition |
| D3 Voice of the customer → CTQ | 4 | At least one customer requirement translated into a measurable CTQ with a target and a specification basis, and the customer named (internal counts) |
| D4 SIPOC and stakeholders | 3 | SIPOC at the right altitude (5–8 steps); stakeholder map with at least one named resistance risk and a planned response |

## Measure — 20 points

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Process map / value stream map | 4 | Current state as observed, not as documented; lead time, %C&A and process cycle efficiency computed with stated basis (calendar vs working time) |
| M2 Data collection plan and operational definitions | 4 | Who, what, when, how, how many; the operational definition would let a stranger collect the same number |
| ★ M3 Measurement system analysis attempted | 6 | Gage R&R or attribute agreement run on the primary metric (or a written, reviewer-accepted reason it is not applicable, e.g., a system timestamp — "we trust the system" is not a reason); result interpreted and, if the system failed, what was done about it |
| M4 Stability before capability | 6 | Baseline control chart appropriate to the data type; stability stated and special causes investigated before capability is claimed; Cp/Cpk or DPMO/sigma with the specification source named |

## Analyze — 25 points

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| A1 Cause generation and narrowing | 6 | Fishbone or equivalent → C&E matrix or FMEA → a short list of checkable causes; the narrowing logic is visible |
| A2 Graphical analysis | 5 | Stratified charts (box plots, multi-vari, scatter) that show where variation lives; charts labeled and read correctly |
| ★ A3 Root cause verified with data | 10 | At least one cause tested with an appropriate method (t-test, ANOVA, chi-square, regression, or a designed comparison) with the practical conclusion written in plain language, assumptions checked, and the effect size stated — a p-value alone is not a verification; a countermeasure aimed at an unverified cause fails this item |
| A4 Honest handling of what did not verify | 4 | Causes that were tested and rejected are shown, not hidden; sample size limits are acknowledged |

## Improve — 20 points

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| I1 Countermeasure selection | 5 | More than one option generated; selection against stated criteria (impact, effort, risk); the chosen countermeasure targets the verified cause |
| I2 Pilot design | 5 | Bounded pilot with a written prediction, a success measure identical to the baseline metric, and a stop rule |
| ★ I3 Before/after evidence | 8 | Same metric, same operational definition, same collection method; control chart or paired comparison showing the shift; effect compared to the prediction; honest reading of partial or unmet results (a well-documented "did not work, here is what we learned and did next" can earn full marks) |
| I4 Mistake-proofing and risk | 2 | Poka-yoke or FMEA-based risk reduction considered; residual risks named |

## Control — 15 points

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| ★ C1 Control plan handed to a named owner | 7 | Control plan with metric, method, frequency, owner, and response plan; process owner has signed; monitoring live for at least 30 days at submission (60 for the `sustained` flag at day 90) |
| C2 Standard work and training | 4 | New standard documented in the vertical's format; the people doing the work were involved in writing it |
| C3 Financial or mission-metric validation | 4 | Benefit classified (hard, soft, cost avoidance, or clinical/service impact); Finance sign-off where money is claimed; no double counting with another project |

## Storytelling — 5 points

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| S1 One-page summary and tollgate narrative | 5 | A stranger can follow problem → cause → change → result in under ten minutes; exhibits over prose; the A3 or storyboard is the record, not a deck |

## Reviewer guidance

- **Mandatory items first.** Read ★ M3, A3, I3, C1 before anything else. If any is unearned,
  the review's job becomes the single most useful sentence about how to earn it.
- **Verified means tested, not asserted.** "The team agreed the cause was X" is A1 evidence,
  not A3. A p-value without a stated effect and a plain-language sentence is not A3 either.
- **Honest failure passes.** A project whose pilot did not move the metric, documented with the
  same rigor and a real next step, can pass. A project reporting a 60% improvement from a
  two-day window cannot.
- **The scope was the sponsor's call.** Score the project that was chartered, not the one you
  would have chosen. Note over-scoping in feedback; do not penalize a completed, bounded slice.
- **Data ethics are a stop.** Evidence of removed inconvenient data points, a changed
  operational definition between before and after, or a baseline reconstructed from memory
  ends the review with a resubmission request and a note to the assessment lead.
- **Feedback format:** two strengths; every ★ item's status in one sentence each; the single
  highest-leverage fix first for a resubmission; the Black Belt invitation where the project
  reveals a Black-Belt-sized problem.
- **Calibration:** all reviewers score the shared anchor set (a clean pass; a high-scoring fail
  on an unverified cause; an honest-failure pass; a data-ethics stop) before their first live
  review and quarterly thereafter; drift greater than 5 points total or any disagreement on a
  ★ item triggers re-calibration. Annual random re-review of 10% of certified projects per the
  audit policy.
