# Sponsor Verification and Finance Validation

*The third certification gate. The sponsor attests that the project happened, that the results
are real and that the control plan is owned; where money is claimed, a Finance partner
validates the figure; where the benefit is clinical or service impact, the executive who owns
that metric validates it. The reviewer scores rubric item C3 from these pages and reads Part A
alongside items I3 and C1 — [`review-rubric.md`](review-rubric.md).*

**When:** at closure, attached to the project package before independent review. The
calculation basis in Part B or Part C is agreed at Tollgate 2 and confirmed at Tollgate 4
([`tollgate-checklists.md`](tollgate-checklists.md)), so nothing here is a surprise.

**Which parts apply**

| Your project claims | Part A | Part B | Part C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Money (hard, soft or cost avoidance) | Sponsor | Finance partner | — |
| Clinical or service impact, no money | Sponsor | — | Mission-metric executive |
| Both | Sponsor | Finance partner | Mission-metric executive |
| Practicum track | Instructor acting as sponsor | Marked *simulated* | Marked *simulated* |

Two rules that hold across all three parts:

- **Nobody signs for themselves.** The Green Belt does not sign Part A. The sponsor does not
  sign Part B unless they are also the organization's finance lead, in which case they say so.
  The Finance partner is not the Green Belt's line manager.
- **Signing a line you cannot stand behind is the one thing not to do.** A partial attestation
  with a plain note ("I did not attend Tollgate 3; my deputy did") is reviewed as written. A
  blank or evasive one goes back to you.

### B1. Benefit classification

Classify every benefit claimed. One project often carries more than one class; keep them in
separate rows and never add classes together into one headline.

| Class | Definition | Counts as | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hard** | Actual spend reduced, or revenue realized, traceable to a ledger line that moved: overtime, scrap, consumables, external fees, penalties paid, purchased services | Validated financial impact | The ledger line before and after, over the evidence window; the unit cost source |
| **Soft** | Time freed or capacity created that is not removed from a budget: hours saved with no change to headcount or overtime; throughput gained without a sale attached | Capacity, reported in hours or units; converted to money only at Finance's stated rate and labeled soft | The time measurement from the project (same definition as the metric) and the rate used |
| **Cost avoidance** | Spend that was budgeted or committed and now will not occur: a planned temporary hire not made, a penalty that would have been incurred under a signed contract, a capital purchase deferred | Avoided spend, labeled as such, never presented as savings against a ledger that did not move | The budget line or commitment, dated before the project, and the decision not to spend |

### B3. Annualization rule

1. The figure that is validated is the **measured benefit over the evidence window**. That
   figure is the one the program reports and the one the badge's verified-impact field
   refers to.
2. An annualized figure may be stated **only alongside** the measured figure, by Finance, by
   run rate: measured per-period benefit × periods per year, capped at 12 months from the
   implementation date, with the basis written next to it ("$1,730 over 8 weeks; run rate
   ≈ $11,200 per year, projected, assumes current volume"). No growth, volume increase or
   roll-out to other lines, sites or units is assumed.
3. The evidence window is at least the post-implementation window used for rubric item I3
   and never shorter than four weeks. A seasonal process states the season and does not
   annualize across seasons it has not measured.
4. The program's published outcomes page carries measured-window figures with their period
   and n; nothing there is annualized or extrapolated
   ([outcomes page](../../program-operations/outcomes-page.md), rule 4).

## What the reviewer does with these pages

- **C3 (4 points).** Full marks: every benefit row classified in a recognized class; the
  calculation basis written and matching the metric's operational definition; Finance
  signature where money is claimed, or the mission-metric signature where it is not; the
  no-double-counting declaration made. A soft benefit presented as hard, a run rate presented
  as measured, or a money claim with no Finance signature each cost points; a benefit with no
  basis at all earns none.
- **I3 and C1.** Part A2 and A3 are read against the before/after evidence and the control
  plan. A discrepancy between the sponsor's account and the package is one question to the
  sponsor before anything else.
- **The badge.** The reviewer records which validation the package carries — Finance,
  mission metric, both, or none claimed — for the credential's verified-impact field, and
  the handover date in A3 as day 0 for the sustainment check.
- **The outcomes page.** Measured-window figures, by class, aggregated per cohort with n and
  period; mission-metric results reported as such and never converted.
- **Practicum track.** Part A is signed by the instructor acting as sponsor and says so; Parts
  B and C are completed against the simulated cost and outcome tables and every figure is
  labeled *simulated*. C3 is scored on classification and basis. Nothing from a Practicum
  project enters the outcomes page's impact figures.
