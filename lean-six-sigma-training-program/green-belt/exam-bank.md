# Green Belt Certification Exam — Item Bank (v1)

**Governed by:** [`../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md`](../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md)

**Administration:** 100 items per form, 3 hours, proctored (online proctoring for individual
candidates, on-site for corporate cohorts). Assembled to the blueprint from the CERT pool
(350 items — the 3.5 × minimum for a 100-item form). Provisional cut **80%** pending the
first modified-Angoff panel. One included retake after a 14-day study period; retake forms
share ≤ 25% of items with prior attempts and never the items previously missed.

**Minimally competent candidate (draft for the Angoff panel):** *Can lead a bounded DMAIC
project on a process they touch: write a cause-free charter with a sponsor, map the process
and measure it with an operational definition and a checked measurement system, state
stability before capability, narrow causes with structured tools and verify the survivors
with the right basic test correctly interpreted, pilot a countermeasure with before/after
evidence, and hand over a control plan — with coaching at each tollgate, and without being
expected to design an experiment, run multiple regression, or lead cross-functional change.*

## Bank status

Every item has been written to the item-writing standards and is awaiting the second-SME
review the policy requires before entering the active bank (policy §2). No item has
empirical statistics yet; p-values and point-biserials arrive with the first cohorts.
Retired and revised items are logged in the program change log.
