# Benchmark Library

*Numbers the program has actually measured, published with their provenance. Nothing in
this library is quoted from a textbook.*

The program's courses say things like "in outpatient clinics we have seen touch-time
ratios of 5–12%." That sentence is only allowed when this library has the row behind it.
Until then the course says what it can honestly say: *"we have not measured this yet."*
Learners and buyers can read both states here.

## Why this exists

Every Lean course quotes the same secondhand figures (the 95% non-value-added number, the
"Toyota does X" anecdotes). A program that has measured its own learners' processes, and
says so with the sample size, has a claim no competitor can copy without doing the work.

## Rules

1. **Provenance or nothing.** Every row carries the source population, the period, the
   sample size, the operational definition, and how it was collected.
2. **Minimum n = 30 observations from ≥ 3 organizations** before a figure is quoted in a
   course. Below that the row is published as *forming* and not quoted.
3. **Learner-reported figures are labeled as such**, separately from figures instructors
   measured on site. The two are never pooled.
4. **No organization is identifiable** from any row. Rows are published by vertical and, where
   n allows, by process family; never by client.
5. **Ranges, not point estimates**, with the interquartile range where n ≥ 30.
6. **Every figure quoted in a course links back to its row.** The row's revision date is the
   content's revision date; when a row changes, the course text is on the change log.
7. Figures are reviewed annually with the BoK crosswalk. A row not refreshed in three years
   is retired from the courses and marked *stale* here.

## What gets measured, and where the data comes from

| Measure | Definition | Source in the program |
|---|---|---|
| Elapsed-vs-value ratio | Value-added time ÷ elapsed time for one work item, learner-observed | White Belt dossier entry 7 (submitted entries only, with consent) |
| Waste-category distribution | Share of submitted observations by DOWNTIME category | White Belt field exercise submissions |
| Search time | Minutes per day a learner reports searching for items/information | White Belt dossier entry 8 (submitted entries only) |
| Triage latency | Days from observation submission to first status change, corporate rollouts | Rollout dashboard |
| Second-observation rate | Share of White Belt holders submitting a second observation within 90 days | Evaluation plan, L3 |
| Just-do-it cycle time | Days from a Yellow Belt problem statement to a checked countermeasure | Yellow Belt mini-project storyboards |
| Baseline-to-check improvement | Relative change in the Yellow Belt project's primary metric | Yellow Belt storyboards (rubric-passed only) |

## How a row is born

1. Field exercise and dossier data arrive tagged by vertical and role; learners consent at
   submission to aggregate use (the terms of certification §7 cover this; declining changes
   nothing about their credential).
2. Monthly, the assessment lead tallies eligible observations per measure.
3. At n ≥ 30 from ≥ 3 organizations the row moves to *forming*; at the next quarterly review
   it is checked for definition drift and published.
4. Course text that has been waiting on the row ("we have not measured this yet") is updated,
   with a change-log entry.
