# Credential Evidence Policy — the credential that shows the work

Every Lean Six Sigma certificate on the market says "passed." This program's badges can
show what the holder actually did. This policy governs how.

**Applies to:** all levels. **Governed by:** the terms of certification (§7 data) and the
credential specification of each level.

---

## 1. What "evidence" means

An evidence item is the applied artifact that the credential's award criteria required,
attached to the badge as an Open Badges 3.0 `evidence` entry, together with the reviewer's
scored feedback where a human reviewed it.

| Level | Evidence item | Reviewer of record shown |
|---|---|---|
| White Belt (Awareness) | The submitted "See a Waste" observation, as reviewed | Named where human-reviewed; otherwise "completeness-checked" |
| Yellow Belt | The A3-lite mini-project storyboard and its rubric scores | Named instructor |
| Green Belt | Project summary (charter, baseline, result, sponsor verification), and the reviewer's report | Named independent reviewer |
| Black Belt | Project abstract, reviewer-of-record report, sustainment check result | Named reviewer of record |
| Master Black Belt | Capstone abstract (already public by policy), panel composition | Panel chair and external examiner |

The badge without evidence is still a valid credential. Evidence is *additional* and
learner-controlled.

**When no human reviewed it.** A White Belt submission outside the sample has no rubric
scores. Its evidence entry shows the artifact and the words *completeness-checked, not
scored*; it never shows a score the program did not give. A learner who wants a scored
record can request review (rubric §1); the request is granted at the program's reviewer
capacity and never changes the credential.

## 2. Consent, in one screen

At the moment of award the learner is shown three choices, in plain words, defaulting to
the most private:

1. **Badge only** (default). Verification page shows: holder, credential, date, criteria
   met, review status, exit-competence statement.
2. **Badge + reviewed summary.** Adds the evidence item *after the program's redaction pass*
   (§3), the reviewer's feedback, and the reviewer of record's name.
3. **Badge + reviewed summary + my organization named.** Only offered when the organization
   has consented in the rollout agreement.

The learner can move between choices at any time from their credential page, in either
direction, and the change takes effect on the verification URL within a day. Withdrawing
evidence never affects the credential.

## 3. Redaction pass

Before any evidence is attachable the program (not the learner) redacts:

- personal names other than the holder's and the reviewer's, including in images;
- customer, patient, supplier and employee identifiers;
- figures the sponsor marked commercially sensitive in the rollout agreement;
- anything the reviewer flagged for escalation (safety, regulatory) — that evidence is never
  published, and the badge shows "evidence withheld by program" with no further detail.

The learner sees the redacted version and approves it before choosing option 2 or 3.

## 4. What the verification page shows

Always: holder name · credential name (with the honest tier parenthetical) · issue date ·
criteria met · `reviewStatus` · exit-competence statement · revocation state.

With evidence: the artifact (rendered, not a file download) · rubric dimensions and scores ·
reviewer's ≤ 4-sentence feedback · reviewer of record's name and their own credential's
verification link · date of review · the program's sampling statement ("this observation
was one of the 10% human-reviewed; the program publishes its sampling rate").

Never: exam scores, item-level answers, attempt counts, reflection text, dossier contents,
anything from a cohort's other learners.

## 5. Reviewers' names

A reviewer of record consents to being named on the credentials they review as a condition
of the reviewer role. Their name links to their own verification page, so a chain of
credentials is inspectable end to end. A reviewer who leaves the program stays named on
credentials already issued; their verification page notes the period of service.

## 6. Employers

The verification page is the employer's view. The program does not answer employer requests
for more than the page shows, and says so on the page. A learner who wants to share more can
change their consent choice; the program never does it for them.

## 7. Revocation

Revocation (terms of certification §4) removes evidence from the verification URL along with
the credential. The URL resolves to "revoked" with a date and nothing else.
