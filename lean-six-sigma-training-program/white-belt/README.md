# White Belt — Foundations of Operational Excellence

**Format:** 100% self-paced e-learning (+ optional live kickoff for corporate cohorts)
**Duration:** 3–4 hours, 12 micro-modules, plus twelve five-minute "go look" tasks done in the
learner's own workplace · **Prerequisites:** none
**Credential:** Lean Six Sigma White Belt (Awareness) — verifiable badge, no expiry, no CEUs

This level is built out across all eight components of the program's delivery model. The
component map below is the index; each component's detail lives in its own document.

---

## Course structure

| # | Module | Time | Interaction |
|---|--------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Why Processes Fail People | 15 min | Reflection prompt |
| 2 | What Is a Process? (SIPOC at awareness level) | 15 min | Click-to-assign SIPOC sort |
| 3 | Where This Came From: TPS, Deming, and Variation | 15 min | 3-question check |
| 4 | Value-Added vs. Non-Value-Added Work | 15 min | Click-to-assign classification **+ cumulative check** |
| 5 | The 8 Wastes, Part 1: D-O-W-N | 20 min | Spot-the-waste video, Segment A |
| 6 | The 8 Wastes, Part 2: T-I-M-E | 20 min | Spot-the-waste video, Segment B **+ cumulative check** |
| 7 | Waste in Your World (vertical anchor story) | 15 min | Vertical scenario quiz |
| 8 | 5S: A Place for Everything | 15 min | Before/after photo pairs **+ cumulative check** |
| 9 | DMAIC as a Storyline | 20 min | Phase assignment, 15 activities |
| 10 | Process Problems vs. People Problems | 15 min | Judgment scenarios **+ cumulative check** |
| 11 | The Belt System and Your Role in CI Culture | 10 min | — |
| 12 | Your First Contribution: the "See a Waste" Exercise | 20 min | Field exercise submission **+ cumulative check** |

**Before Module 1:** 10-item pre-assessment (test-out of Modules 1–6 at 9/10; pre/post gain
reported per cohort). **After every module:** a five-minute *go look* task in the workplace,
recorded in the learner's [process dossier](templates/process-dossier.md) — by Module 12 the
learner has found their first three improvement opportunities. **After Module 12:** knowledge
check, then field exercise (one dossier entry, through the rubric coach) → badge.

## Certification requirements

1. All 12 modules completed.
2. Knowledge check passed (20 items from the 80-item bank; provisional 70% cut pending the
   modified-Angoff panel; unlimited retakes, ≤ 25% item overlap, no time limit).
3. Field exercise submitted past the completeness gate — and, where sampled (10% individual /
   100% corporate), scoring ≥ 5/8 with no dimension at 0.

## Vertical variants

Modules 5–7 ship in three flavors sharing one teaching skeleton; only footage, click-targets
and the anchor story change. Corporate deployments pick a default; individual learners choose
at enrollment and may switch without losing progress. Scripts:
[`vertical-variants.md`](vertical-variants.md).
