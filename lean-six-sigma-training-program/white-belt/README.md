# White Belt — Foundations of Operational Excellence

**Format:** 100% self-paced e-learning · **Duration:** 3–4 hours (12 micro-modules)
**Prerequisites:** None · **Credential:** Awareness badge (digital, verifiable metadata)

## Course structure

| # | Module | Time | Assessment touchpoint |
|---|--------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | Why Processes Fail People | 15 min | Reflection prompt |
| 2 | What Is a Process? (SIPOC at awareness level) | 15 min | Drag-and-drop SIPOC |
| 3 | Where This Came From: TPS, Deming, and Variation | 15 min | 3-question check |
| 4 | Value-Added vs. Non-Value-Added Work | 15 min | Sort-the-activities exercise |
| 5 | The 8 Wastes, Part 1: D-O-W-N | 20 min | Spot-the-waste video 1 |
| 6 | The 8 Wastes, Part 2: T-I-M-E | 20 min | Spot-the-waste video 2 |
| 7 | Waste in Your World (vertical variants) | 15 min | Vertical scenario quiz |
| 8 | 5S: A Place for Everything | 15 min | Before/after photo pairs |
| 9 | DMAIC as a Storyline | 20 min | Phase-matching exercise |
| 10 | Process Problems vs. People Problems | 15 min | Scenario judgment items |
| 11 | The Belt System and Your Role in CI Culture | 10 min | — |
| 12 | Your First Contribution: the "See a Waste" Exercise | 20 min | Field exercise submission |

Then: **Knowledge check** (20 questions assembled to blueprint from an 80-item tagged bank;
provisional 70% cut pending the modified-Angoff panel; unlimited retakes with ≤ 25% item
overlap) and **field exercise submission** → badge issued.

Also built in: a **10-item pre-assessment** before Module 1 (test-out of Modules 1–6 at
9/10; pre/post learning gain reported per corporate cohort) and **cumulative checks** at
the end of Modules 4, 6, 8, 10, and 12 that re-test earlier modules (spaced retrieval).
All governed by [`../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md`](../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md).

## Vertical variants

Modules 5–7 ship in three flavors sharing one script skeleton — only the examples and the
spot-the-waste video change:
- **Manufacturing & Supply Chain** (plant floor walkthrough)
- **Healthcare** (outpatient clinic walkthrough)
- **Transactional/Services** (order-to-cash office walkthrough)

Corporate deployments pick a default flavor; individual learners choose at enrollment.
