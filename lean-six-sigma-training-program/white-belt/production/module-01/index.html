Glossary

Continuous improvement (CI)
The ongoing practice of improving how work is done, usually in many small steps rather than a few large ones.
Process
Any repeatable sequence of steps that turns inputs into outputs for a customer. Covered properly in Module 2.
Special cause
A source of variation that is unusual and identifiable: one person, one machine, one day. Contrasted with common cause, the ordinary variation built into how a process is designed. You work with both properly at Yellow Belt.
The system
Everything about how work is designed and managed: layout, tools, forms, software, schedules, policies, incentives, and training.
