How this is reviewed

Every submission passes a completeness check. One in ten (all of them, in a corporate rollout) is then scored by a calibrated human on four dimensions, each 0, 1 or 2. Pass is 5 of 8 with no dimension at 0.

D1 Real and specific — a named process, place and recognizable moment. D2 Observational, not judgmental — reads like a camera recorded it. D3 Waste correctly identified — the category fits. D4 Impact named — who receives this step's output and what they experience.

The optional size and idea are never scored and never penalized. Grammar and spelling are not dimensions. A three-line observation from the shop floor that locates a real problem scores full marks.

If the blame flag is still showing when you submit, your observation goes to a human reviewer regardless of sampling, and you'll be told so. That's protection, not punishment.
