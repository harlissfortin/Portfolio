### Worked example (transactional flavor)

> **Where:** Invoice processing, accounts payable.
> **What I observed:** Invoices arriving by email are printed, then the same details are
> typed into the ERP system by hand. Roughly one in ten comes back from the approver because
> a field was mistyped, and it is corrected and re-entered.
> **Wastes:** Extra-processing (print + retype), Defects (mistyped fields), Waiting
> (correction loop adds 1–2 days).
> **Who it affects:** Suppliers are paid late on the corrected invoices and call to chase;
> the AP team spends part of each afternoon on corrections.
> **Rough size:** ~60 invoices/day; corrections affect ~6/day at ~15 minutes each.
> **Idea:** I wonder if the email attachments could go straight into the ERP intake instead
> of being printed first.
