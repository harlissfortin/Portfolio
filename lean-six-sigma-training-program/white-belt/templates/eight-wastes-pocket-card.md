# The 8 Wastes Pocket Card (DOWNTIME)

*Print double-sided at badge size, or save to your phone. Side 1: the wastes. Side 2: the
questions that find them.*

---

## Side 1 — Name it

| | Waste | One-line definition | Spot it as… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D** | Defects | Wrong, incomplete, or unusable work | Rework, corrections, scrap, returns, apologies |
| **O** | Overproduction | More / sooner / faster than needed | Big batches "while we're at it," reports nobody asked for |
| **W** | Waiting | People or work sitting idle | Queues, approval limbo, "waiting on…" |
| **N** | Non-utilized talent | Skills and ideas going unused | "Nobody ever asked me," experts doing data entry |
| **T** | Transportation | The **work** moving between places/systems | Couriers, handoffs, re-entering data across systems |
| **I** | Inventory | Work piling up between steps | Stockpiles, backlogs, full inboxes, WIP |
| **M** | Motion | The **person** moving | Walking, reaching, searching, six screens per task |
| **E** | Extra-processing | More than the customer requires | Gold-plating, double-checking, sign-offs that add nothing |

**Memory hooks:** Transportation moves the *work*; Motion moves the *worker*.
Overproduction is the waste that breeds the others. Rework is never value-added.

---

## Side 2 — Find it

Walk any process and ask:

1. Where does work **wait**? (longest queue = biggest lever)
2. What gets **redone** or corrected? How often?
3. What do we make/do that the next step **didn't ask for**?
4. Where do people **walk, search, or click** more than the work requires?
5. What **piles up** — and what is the pile hiding?
6. How many times does the work **change hands or systems**?
7. What steps exist "because we've always done it"? Who **uses** their output?
8. What do the people doing this work **already know** would fix it?

**The value test (all three or it's waste):**
☐ Customer values it ☐ It changes the work item ☐ Done right the first time
