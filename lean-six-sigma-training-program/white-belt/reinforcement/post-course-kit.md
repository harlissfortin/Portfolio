# White Belt — Reinforcement & Alumni Kit

A 3.5-hour awareness course decays fast on its own. This is the designed decay-resistance:
what the learner receives after the badge, on what schedule, and why each piece exists.

Design rule: **every touchpoint asks for or reports on an observation.** None of them are
newsletters. A reinforcement asset that only tells is a marketing email wearing a lab coat.

---

## 1. The 12-month sequence

| When | Asset | Ask | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Badge, PDF, pocket card, rubric feedback (where reviewed) | — | Completion, and the feedback that teaches |
| Day 3 | **"One waste, one week"** — a 90-second micro-lesson on a single waste in their vertical | Notice one instance this week | Retrieval while memory is warm |
| Day 7 | Peer observation of the week from their own organization (corporate) or from the vertical library (individual) | Reply with one you have seen | Social proof that others are doing it |
| Day 14 | **Waste Walk invitation** — the checklist plus a 5-minute how-to | Walk one area for 30 minutes | Converts knowledge into a repeatable habit |
| Day 30 | Theme report: what the organization surfaced and what happened (corporate); "what White Belts found this month" (individual) | Notice whether yours is in it | The closing of the loop — the retention driver |
| Day 45 | **"Is it a Just-Do-It?"** — the Yellow Belt triage test, taught as a 2-minute tool | Try it on your own observation | First genuine taste of the next level |
| Day 60 | Vertical case: a small improvement, start to finish, with the numbers | — | Shows what "done" looks like |
| Day 90 | **90-day pulse** (3 items) + second-observation invitation | Submit another observation | The Level-3 behavior metric, and the habit checkpoint |
| Day 120 | Yellow Belt invitation, personalized where their observation or rubric feedback flagged it | Consider enrolling | Progression — timed after the habit is either formed or not |
| Month 6 | **"Waste of the Month"** cadence begins: one 3-minute vertical case, monthly | Occasional reply prompts | Long-tail contact, low cost |
| Month 12 | Anniversary: their own first observation replayed back, with what happened to it | Refresher offer; Yellow Belt offer | The single most effective progression touch — people are moved by their own year-old words |

Cadence discipline: nothing between day 120 and month 6 except the monthly case. Over-mailing
kills the list, and the list is the Yellow Belt funnel.

## 2. The "Waste of the Month" format

Three minutes, one page or one short video, same skeleton every month so it is skimmable:
**the situation → the waste named → what one person noticed → what changed → the number.**
Sourced from the program's own project library and (with permission) from client rollouts,
rotated across the three verticals. Always attributes the noticing to a named frontline
person, never to a belt — that is the whole point of the asset.

## 3. Community

Free tier for every White Belt holder, and deliberately shallow: a read-and-post forum, the
template library, the monthly case, and a monthly open "tool clinic" webinar (recorded).
No paid tier at this level — a White Belt paying a monthly fee is a bad-faith product, and the
free tier exists to feed Yellow Belt enrollment, not to be monetized directly.

Moderation matters more here than at any other level: the forum's norm is *describe the
process, never the person or the employer*. Posts naming employers as villains get edited to
anonymize, with a note explaining why. One unmoderated thread of employer-bashing makes the
forum unusable for every corporate client simultaneously.

## 4. Manager reinforcement (corporate)

Managers get their own two touches, because learner habit dies fastest when the manager's
response is flat:
- **Day 10:** three questions to ask in a team meeting, and the one sentence to open with
  ("thank you — tell me more").
- **Day 45:** their department's observation themes, with the triage status, and a prompt to
  close the loop verbally with the people who submitted them.

## 5. Refresher

A paid 45-minute **White Belt Refresher** micro-course, offered at month 12 and to anyone
re-entering a CI role: the eight wastes re-drilled, what has changed in the organization
since, and one new observation required. Cheap, evergreen, and it keeps the credential
feeling alive without inventing a recertification requirement that an awareness badge cannot
honestly justify.

## 6. What is deliberately NOT offered

- **No recertification and no expiry.** The badge is evergreen (per the credential spec).
  Charging an awareness-credential holder to renew would be revenue extracted against the
  program's own integrity claims.
- **No CEUs.** See the credential spec.
- **No "advanced White Belt."** The next step is Yellow Belt. Inventing intermediate tiers to
  sell is belt inflation, which the program's own pitfall analysis names as a way these
  businesses die.
