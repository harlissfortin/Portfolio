# Black Belt Certification Exam — Item Bank (v1)

**Governed by:** [`../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md`](../assessment/standard-setting-and-item-policy.md)

**Administration:** 150 items per form, 4 hours, proctored, **open notes, closed internet**.
Assembled to the blueprint from the CERT pool (525 items — the 3.5 × minimum for a
150-item form). Provisional cut **80%** pending the first modified-Angoff panel. One included
retake after a 14-day study period; retake forms share ≤ 25% of items with prior attempts and
never the items previously missed.

**Minimally competent candidate (draft for the Angoff panel):** *Can lead a cross-functional
improvement project to verified impact with a team they do not manage: choose and justify a
statistical method and recognize when none is warranted, design and analyze a factorial
experiment including a fractional one, state non-normal capability correctly, choose and
interpret the right control chart at scale, redesign a value stream for flow, diagnose
resistance and adjust a stakeholder strategy, run a tollgate as a reviewer, and build a
benefit case Finance will sign — without being expected to design a deployment, teach a
course, or run response-surface optimization unaided.*

## Bank status

Sections A, B and D have had the second-SME review the policy requires; the review note sits
at the foot of each of those section files. Items in Sections C, E1, E2, F, G, H and I are
still awaiting that review before entering the active bank (policy §2). No empirical
statistics yet for any section. Open-notes administration means items that can be answered by
looking up a definition are defects; the second SME rejects them.
