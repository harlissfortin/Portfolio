# Black Belt — Advanced Practitioner & Change Leader

**Format:** hybrid, 16 weeks — ≈ 4 h/week self-paced + weekly 2.5 h live-virtual lab + two
2-day immersion events (a DOE practicum and a change-leadership/facilitation practicum;
in person strongly encouraged, virtual possible) · corporate option: 4 × 4-day on-site waves
with virtual coaching between · **Time:** ≈ 100 h structured + 100–150 h project work ·
**Cohort cap:** 16 per instructor
**Prerequisites:** certified Green Belt (this program, or external plus the calibration
assessment in [`bok-crosswalk-and-credential.md`](bok-crosswalk-and-credential.md) §2.9 —
gaps are bridged with targeted Green Belt modules, never waived) + one completed
DMAIC project + sponsor commitment for a Black-Belt-scale project (cross-functional,
strategically linked, target impact ≥ $75K annualized or an equivalent clinical or
service-level impact).
**Credential:** Certified Lean Six Sigma Black Belt — five gates, a **named Master Black Belt
as reviewer of record on every certificate**. 10 IACET CEUs pending accreditation. Aligned to
the IASSC Black Belt body of knowledge (crosswalk published).

---

## The 16-week calendar

| Weeks | Module | What the candidate does on their project |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **M1 Strategic Define:** project portfolios and strategic linkage; advanced VOC (Kano, conjoint awareness); charter at scale; team formation without authority | Charter signed; strategic linkage statement; stakeholder strategy canvas; team launched |
| 3 | **M2 Measurement systems, advanced:** destructive and attribute-complex MSA; nested and crossed designs; measurement uncertainty | MSA plan for every primary and secondary metric |
| 4–5 | **M3 Data foundations:** distribution identification; transformations; non-normal capability; power and sample size; confidence and prediction | Baseline capability stated with the right distribution; sample sizes justified |
| 6–7 | **M4 Modeling:** multiple regression with diagnostics; binary logistic regression; non-parametrics (Mann-Whitney, Kruskal-Wallis, Mood's median); two-way ANOVA and GLM awareness; **when not to model** | Causes verified with the right model; residual diagnostics shown |
| 8–11 | **M5 Design of experiments (the centerpiece, ≈ 20% of course time):** full factorials; fractional factorials and confounding; blocking; center points and curvature; sequential experimentation; response surface introduction. **Immersion 1: the DOE practicum** (week 10) | An experiment designed, run and analyzed — on the project where possible, on the practicum apparatus always |
| 12 | **M6 Advanced SPC:** chart selection at scale; rational subgrouping; EWMA and CUSUM; short-run SPC | Control strategy for the project's metrics designed |
| 13–14 | **M7 Lean at system level:** future-state value stream design; flow and pull; Little's Law applied; line balancing and workload leveling; setup reduction; TPM/OEE or demand-capacity management; theory of constraints basics; **kaizen event leadership** | Kaizen event scheduled (requirement 4) |
| 15–16 | **M8 Leading change and coaching:** stakeholder strategy at executive level; resistance diagnosis; influence without authority; facilitation skills; executive communication and storytelling with data; coaching Green Belts and running a tollgate from the reviewer's chair; program economics (CoPQ, benefit classes, Finance partnership); DFSS/DMADV awareness; digital CI awareness. **Immersion 2: change-leadership practicum** (week 15) | Executive readout rehearsed; two Green Belt tollgate reviews scheduled (requirement 5) |

Monthly 1:1 coaching with a Master Black Belt (six sessions included) runs from week 1 to
project closure. Project closure may extend to **9 months** after week 16.

## Certification gates (all five required)

1. **Exam:** 150 items, 4 hours, proctored, **open notes, closed internet** (mirrors practice:
   judgment, not recall). Assembled from a **525-item tagged bank** (3.5 × form length).
   Provisional cut **80%** pending the modified-Angoff panel. One included retake after 14
   days; ≤ 25% overlap, never the items previously missed. Bank: [`exam-bank.md`](exam-bank.md).
2. **Capstone project:** scored against the **150-point rubric** by an independent Master
   Black Belt reviewer who did not coach the candidate; Finance and sponsor validation of
   impact. Pass = **120 with every mandatory item earned** (verified root cause; experimental
   or piloted solution evidence; control plan live ≥ 60 days; Finance sign-off; leadership
   evidence). One revise-and-resubmit cycle. Rubric: [`project/review-rubric.md`](project/review-rubric.md).
3. **DOE practicum:** designs, runs, analyzes and presents an experiment end to end on the
   physical apparatus (catapult or paper-helicopter kit, shipped) or the process simulator
   (transactional cohorts); observed and rubric-scored pass/redo.
   [`practicum/doe-practicum.md`](practicum/doe-practicum.md).
4. **Kaizen facilitation:** leads one rapid-improvement event at their employer or a
   partner-pool organization, observed or video-reviewed against the facilitation rubric.
   [`practicum/kaizen-facilitation.md`](practicum/kaizen-facilitation.md).
5. **Green Belt coaching:** formally reviews two Green Belt tollgates using the program rubric,
   to the reviewer standard. [`practicum/green-belt-tollgate-reviews.md`](practicum/green-belt-tollgate-reviews.md).

**Reviewer of record.** A named Master Black Belt signs each certificate and is listed on the
verification page. A human reputation is attached to every credential.

**Minimally competent candidate (draft for the Angoff panel):** *Can lead a cross-functional
improvement project to verified impact with a team they do not manage: choose and justify a
statistical method and recognize when none is warranted, design and analyze a factorial
experiment including a fractional one, state non-normal capability correctly, choose and
interpret the right control chart at scale, redesign a value stream for flow, diagnose
resistance and adjust a stakeholder strategy, run a tollgate as a reviewer, and build a
benefit case Finance will sign — without being expected to design a deployment, teach a
course, or run response-surface optimization unaided.*

## Exam blueprint (150-item form · 525-item bank)

| Section | Per form | In bank | Objectives |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Strategic Define: selection portfolios, strategic linkage, advanced VOC, charter at scale, team launch | 12 | 42 | B1 |
| B. Measurement systems, advanced | 10 | 35 | B2 |
| C. Data foundations: distributions, transformations, non-normal capability, power and sample size, intervals | 18 | 63 | B3 |
| D. Modeling: multiple and logistic regression, diagnostics, non-parametrics, two-way ANOVA/GLM, when not to model | 24 | 84 | B4 |
| E1. DOE: factorial design and analysis, effects, interactions, residuals, operating windows | 15 | 53 | B5 |
| E2. DOE: fractional factorials, confounding and resolution, blocking, center points and curvature, sequential strategy, RSM introduction | 15 | 52 | B5 |
| F. Advanced SPC: chart selection, rational subgrouping, EWMA/CUSUM, short-run | 12 | 42 | B6 |
| G. Lean at system level: future-state VSM, flow and pull, Little's Law, balancing and leveling, setup reduction, TPM/OEE, demand-capacity, constraints, kaizen leadership | 18 | 63 | B7 |
| H. Leading change: stakeholder strategy, resistance, influence, facilitation, executive communication, coaching and tollgate review | 14 | 49 | B8 |
| I. Program economics: CoPQ, benefit classification, Finance partnership; DFSS/DMADV awareness; digital CI awareness | 12 | 42 | B9, B10 |

Constraints: heavy exhibit interpretation (≈ 45% X), ≈ 35% scenario judgment (S), ≈ 20%
concept (K); ≥ 60% Apply/Analyze/Evaluate; vertical rule; balanced keys; no two items sharing
a scenario on one form; "when not to use it" items required in C, D, E and F.

**Tag format:** `[section · objective · Bloom · vertical · type · pool]`
Objectives: **B1** select and charter strategically linked projects; advanced VOC · **B2**
design and interpret advanced measurement systems · **B3** identify distributions, transform,
state non-normal capability, size samples · **B4** build and diagnose regression and logistic
models; choose non-parametrics; recognize when not to model · **B5** design, run and analyze
experiments incl. fractional, blocked, with curvature; sequential strategy · **B6** deploy
advanced SPC · **B7** design flow at system level; lead kaizen events · **B8** lead change,
facilitate, coach Green Belts and review tollgates · **B9** build the financial case with
Finance · **B10** DFSS and digital CI awareness.
