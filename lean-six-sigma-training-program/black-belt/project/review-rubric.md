# Black Belt Capstone Project — Review Rubric (150 points)

**Pass = 120 points with every mandatory item (★) earned.** Scored by an independent Master
Black Belt reviewer who did not coach the candidate; the reviewer of record signs the
certificate. Feedback within 20 business days; one revise-and-resubmit cycle included.
Published in week 1.

---

## Define and strategic linkage — 20

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| D1 Problem and opportunity statement | 4 | Cause-free, blame-free, quantified; the gap is stated in the business's own metric |
| D2 Strategic linkage | 6 | A written line from the project's primary metric to a stated organizational objective; the sponsor confirms the linkage in the charter |
| D3 Scope and cross-functional charter | 5 | Two or more functions in scope; decision rights and escalation path written; the candidate leads people they do not manage |
| D4 Advanced VOC | 5 | Customer requirements gathered by a stated method (interviews, Kano survey, complaint analysis), translated to CTQs with targets and specification basis |

## Measure — 20

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Measurement systems | 8 | MSA appropriate to each primary metric's data type and situation (destructive, attribute-complex, or system-generated with a validation check); results interpreted; inadequate systems fixed before baseline |
| M2 Baseline with the right distribution | 8 | Stability shown on the right chart with rational subgrouping justified; capability stated with the distribution identified and, where needed, transformed or fitted; sample size justified by a power or precision calculation |
| M3 Data provenance | 4 | Where every dataset came from, how it was cleaned, and what was excluded and why — auditable |

## Analyze — 30

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| A1 Cause structure | 6 | From a wide cause set to a testable few, with the narrowing logic (C&E matrix, FMEA, graphical stratification) shown |
| ★ A2 Root cause verified with an appropriate method | 16 | Method matched to the question and the data (regression with diagnostics, logistic, non-parametric, designed comparison, or experiment); assumptions checked; effect size and practical significance stated in plain language; alternative explanations addressed. A model without diagnostics, or a p-value without an effect, does not earn this |
| A3 Restraint | 4 | At least one place where the candidate chose *not* to model or test, with the reason (insufficient data, question answerable graphically, measurement system too weak) |
| A4 Rejected hypotheses shown | 4 | What was tested and did not verify is in the record |

## Improve — 30

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| ★ I1 Experimental or piloted solution evidence | 14 | Either a designed experiment on the process (design justified, analysis correct, operating window derived) or a controlled pilot with a written prediction, a comparison group or period, and the same metric as baseline; result compared to prediction; honest reading of partial or unmet outcomes |
| I2 Solution design and risk | 6 | Options generated and selected against criteria; FMEA on the new process; mistake-proofing designed in |
| I3 Flow and system design (where applicable) | 5 | Future-state map or capacity model with the arithmetic (takt, Little's Law, balance) shown; if not applicable, a written reason |
| I4 Implementation management | 5 | Plan with owners and dates; deviations recorded; the people doing the work involved in designing the change |

## Control — 20

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| ★ C1 Control plan live ≥ 60 days | 10 | Control plan with the right chart and response plan; process owner signed; at least 60 days of live monitoring at submission with the chart shown; sustainment mechanism owned |
| C2 Standard work and capability transfer | 5 | Standards written with the people who do the work; training completed and recorded; the owner can explain the chart without the candidate |
| C3 Control strategy at scale | 5 | Chart choice justified (including EWMA/CUSUM or short-run where the situation calls for it); subgrouping and sampling frequency reasoned |

## Financial validation — 10

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| ★ F1 Finance sign-off | 6 | Benefits classified (hard, soft, cost avoidance, revenue, mission/clinical); calculation basis written; Finance partner named and signed; no double counting with other projects |
| F2 Cost of the project | 4 | Project costs stated and netted; annualization basis stated; no extrapolation beyond the evidence window without a labeled assumption |

## Leadership evidence — 15

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| ★ L1 Team and stakeholder navigation documented | 8 | Stakeholder strategy canvas at start and at least one revision; a documented resistance episode with the diagnosis and what the candidate did; team decisions recorded; a sponsor statement on how the candidate led |
| L2 Executive communication | 4 | Executive readout delivered (recorded or observed) in ≤ 15 minutes, one page, decisions requested and obtained |
| L3 Coaching | 3 | Evidence of developing at least one team member or Green Belt during the project |

## Storytelling — 5

| Item | Pts | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| S1 The record | 5 | A stranger can follow the project from the A3 and appendices alone; exhibits over prose; every chart labeled with its data source |

## Reviewer guidance

- **★ items first:** A2, I1, C1, F1, L1. A 135-point project with a model and no diagnostics
  fails on A2 and the review says exactly how to earn it.
- **Method fit over method sophistication.** A two-sample comparison that answered the
  question correctly outscores a multiple regression that did not need to exist. A3 rewards
  restraint on purpose.
- **Finance is not a formality.** Without a named Finance partner's sign-off where money is
  claimed, F1 is unearned and the project is not certifiable; mission-metric projects
  (clinical, safety, service level) use the sponsor's executive equivalent, named.
- **Leadership evidence is evidence.** A sentence that says "stakeholders were managed" is
  not L1. A revised canvas, a dated note on a resistance episode, and a sponsor's statement are.
- **Honest failure passes; extrapolated success does not.**
- **Data ethics are a stop**, as at Green Belt, with a note to the assessment lead.
- **Feedback format:** two strengths; each ★ item's status in a sentence; highest-leverage fix
  first; the Master Black Belt fork or the specialist fork named where the project points to it.
- **Calibration:** anchor set of four (clean pass; high-score fail on an undiagnosed model;
  honest-failure pass; Finance-unsigned fail) before first review and quarterly; any ★
  disagreement re-calibrates. Annual 10% re-review per the audit policy.
